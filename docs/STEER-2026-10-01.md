## VU1 — my earlier brief was wrong in the other direction. This replaces it.

**Upstream VU1 is not unfinished.** PR #191 *"refactor: refactor VU1"* merged 2026-08-05; its own
words were *"current VU1 was almost all wrong"*. It is now a full cycle-modelled interpreter with a
decode cache (PR #158) and **40 test cases**.

The MAC/STATUS/CLIP defects from #189 are **superseded and already fixed on main**. Do not
cherry-pick #189 — take the delta from current `main`.

Two things are still real:
- **PR #200 (unmerged):** a reordered instruction pair does not guard `Q`, so a `DIV`, `SQRT` or
  `RSQRT` in the lower half still feeds the upper half the `Q` the lower half just produced. The
  general fix needs no hazard table: **compute the upper half into locals, run the lower half, then
  commit.**
- **#161 (open, unaddressed):** per-opcode tests. The 40 existing cases are behavioural/latency, not
  one per instruction.

**The route is the captain's decision, not yours.** The most tractable path is linking **PCSX2's
microVU** as a static library, the way `drakolordx7/KillzoneRecomp` does. The seam is documented and
narrow: point `PS2Memory::m_vu1Code` / `m_vu1Data` at microVU's arrays — which **must live inside the
executable image**, because microVU encodes VU addresses as displacements from its own text pointer —
then invalidate with one hook comparing `getVU1CodeGeneration()` in the MSCAL callback.

It is the only route that is *complete* rather than *census-gated*: `nathanialf/ico-recomp`
hard-errors on any opcode outside five measured programs and already rejects `OPMULA`/`OPMSUB`, which
GT4 will certainly use.

**The costs, stated honestly: MSVC x64 only, AVX2 required, and linking PCSX2 is GPL-3.0-or-later.**
That is a licence decision for the captain as well as a technical one. Whether microVU builds under
GCC or Clang on Linux is **unverified**, and that test is cheap — it is the first thing to establish
if he says yes. **Until then, do not start a VU1 port.**

Three ideas from ico-recomp stand regardless of route: compute-upper, run-lower, then commit; **print
what you did not model** (Law 2's honest-reporting posture); and bind recompiled microprograms by
content hash, with an unknown hash a loud fatal, never a skip.
