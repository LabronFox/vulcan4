## 3. The wall as it stands at the end of the day (the resume point)

**SIF0 DMA chain routing.** From `docs/W277-RESULTS.md` §R30ar, measured:

- `pcsx2/Sif0.cpp:88` → `sif0ch.madr = tag[1]`: on real hardware the **SIF0 destination is the game's
  DMA-chain tag MADR**, not the IOP's descriptor.
- Our guest stores `0x20874300` to `0x654a84` (its SIF0 receive MADR = `0x00874300`) and then clears it.
- The IOP's one `SifSetDma` writes **`dst = 0x8851c0`** — a different buffer, 0x80 bytes. The command
  never lands at `0x00874300`, so the dispatcher at `0x5608e0` busy-polls a tag that cannot arrive.
- **Missing mechanism:** the EE-side SIF0 DMA chain (MADR/tag) routing from the game's CHCR — our
  runtime does not model it at all.

**Next action, named:** port `pcsx2/Sif0.cpp` (`HandleEETransfer` / `sif0ch.madr = tag[1]`) into
`ps2xRuntime`'s SIF0 path, then re-capture. Nothing else is on the critical path.
